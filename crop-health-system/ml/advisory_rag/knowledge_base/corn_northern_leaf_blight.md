# Corn (Maize) — Northern Leaf Blight (Exserohilum turcicum)

## Overview
Long, cigar-shaped gray-green to tan lesions on leaves, starting on lower leaves and moving upward. Significant yield loss possible if it reaches the ear leaf before grain fill.

## Favorable conditions
Moderate temperatures (18-27°C) with extended periods of high humidity or leaf wetness; disease carries over on infected crop residue.

## Integrated pest & disease management actions
- Rotate away from corn for at least one season in heavily affected fields; the pathogen survives on residue.
- Consider residue management (incorporation) to speed breakdown of infected debris.
- Select resistant hybrids for future planting where available.

## Safe input use
Foliar fungicide can be justified when lesions reach the ear leaf before grain fill in susceptible hybrids — follow label rate and timing; not always economically justified for lower-leaf-only infections late in the season.

## When to refer to an expert or lab
If lesions are progressing rapidly up the plant before tasseling, get an extension assessment of economic spray threshold for your specific hybrid and growth stage.

## Follow-up monitoring
Weekly checks from knee-high stage through grain fill, tracking how far up the plant lesions have progressed.
