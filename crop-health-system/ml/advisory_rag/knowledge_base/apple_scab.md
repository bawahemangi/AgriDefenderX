# Apple — Scab (Venturia inaequalis)

## Overview
Olive-green to black velvety spots on leaves and fruit, which can cause premature leaf drop and corky, cracked lesions on fruit that reduce marketability.

## Favorable conditions
Cool, wet spring weather during leaf-out and early fruit development; infection is driven by leaf-wetness duration after rain.

## Integrated pest & disease management actions
- Rake and destroy fallen leaves in autumn/winter — this is where the fungus overwinters and reinfects in spring.
- Prune to improve airflow and light penetration through the canopy.
- Choose scab-resistant varieties for new plantings where feasible.

## Safe input use
Protectant fungicide programs timed to rain events during the early season (green tip through petal fall) are the standard approach; follow label timing and dosage precisely, since scab fungicide programs are schedule-sensitive.

## When to refer to an expert or lab
For orchards with a history of scab, involve extension services in building a season-long spray timing plan based on local weather forecasting.

## Follow-up monitoring
Inspect after each rain event during the susceptible early-season window.
