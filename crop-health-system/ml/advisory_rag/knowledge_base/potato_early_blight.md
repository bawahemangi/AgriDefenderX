# Potato — Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

## Overview
Dark, concentric "target-spot" lesions on lower/older leaves first, which can coalesce and cause premature defoliation, reducing tuber yield and size.

## Favorable conditions
Warm temperatures (24-29°C), alternating wet/dry periods, plant stress from low fertility or moisture stress, and dense canopy with poor airflow.

## Integrated pest & disease management actions
- Remove and destroy heavily infected foliage; avoid overhead irrigation.
- Maintain balanced fertility — potassium-deficient and drought-stressed plants show more severe symptoms.
- Rotate out of potato/tomato for at least 2 seasons in affected plots.
- Hill soil properly around the base to reduce splash-driven spread to tubers.

## Safe input use
Protectant or systemic fungicides labeled for potato early blight can be used preventively as canopy closes, especially in fields with prior history. Follow label rate and pre-harvest interval strictly; rotate fungicide groups across the season to manage resistance risk.

## When to refer to an expert or lab
If lesions appear on more than 25% of foliage before tuber bulking is complete, or tuber lesions are suspected, refer to the extension officer — yield impact at this stage can be significant.

## Follow-up monitoring
Re-check weekly through the growing season, more frequently during warm, humid spells.
